Fetch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Fetch is an AI personal assistant that helps users discover and coordinate products, services, and mobility through connected providers and Fetch partner services.

Information we process

Depending on the services a user chooses, Fetch may process information needed to fulfill a request, such as account information, contact details, delivery or pickup locations, service preferences, order or ride details, and information returned by connected providers.

Connected services

When a user connects a third-party service, Fetch uses the permissions granted by that user to perform the requested action. For example, a connected mobility provider may provide information needed to request or manage a ride. Fetch does not ask for permissions beyond what is needed for the requested integration.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Fetch, authenticate connected services, fulfill user requests, communicate service status, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical issues.

Sharing

Fetch may transmit information to the third-party provider selected by the user when that transmission is necessary to fulfill the user's request. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to applicable requirements and operational needs.

Your choices

Users can choose whether to connect a third-party service and can stop using a connected service. Requests concerning personal information can be directed to the support contact associated with the Fetch service.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Fetch support team through the contact details provided by Fetch.

This page describes Fetch's intended data practices and may be updated as the product evolves.
